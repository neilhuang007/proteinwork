Biology lead submission review — 18 October 2026

My assessment is that the submission shows concrete progress in inspecting and documenting a biological dataset. The linked CSV provides evidence beyond the report's narrative and screenshots: its position counts and several summary statistics are internally consistent. The work establishes an alignment-inspection stage, while the handoff needed for the planned protein sampler remains incomplete. I did not run the author's full pipeline, so its claimed end-to-end validation is still unverified.

I read all five pages of the supplied PDF, inspected the code and table screenshots, and downloaded the linked `kunitz_position_stats.csv`. The file contains 335 rows and 21 columns. The following checks were calculated directly from that CSV, independently of the report's summary table:

| Claim or check | What I could establish |
| --- | --- |
| 335 stored alignment columns | The CSV has exactly 335 rows, numbered consecutively from 1 to 335. |
| 43,623 sequence records | Every row's gap and non-gap counts sum to 43,623. This confirms the CSV's internal denominator, not the count in the unavailable original alignment. |
| 53 columns with uppercase standard amino-acid observations | Exactly 53 rows have a positive `standard_amino_acid_count`. |
| 10 columns with at least 90% consensus | Exactly 10 rows have `most_common_frequency >= 0.9` under the file's stated uppercase-observation convention. |
| Gap and frequency arithmetic | Every gap count equals its dot count plus its hyphen count. Gap frequencies, standard-observation fractions, and both cysteine-frequency fields agree with their corresponding counts. |
| Nonstandard symbols | The per-position unexpected-character counts total 143, consistent with the report's 1 + 88 + 54. The CSV does not independently establish the B/X/x breakdown. |
| 18,333 duplicates beyond first copies | Not verifiable from a per-position table. This needs the sequence records or duplicate output. |
| Full independent validation passed | Reported in the PDF, but the complete scripts, original input, and validation log were not included in the linked artifact. |

Preserving the downloaded representation is a sensible choice at this stage. Pfam documents different meanings for hyphens and dots, and uses lowercase residues for insert states. Consequently, the 53-column result is a count under the script's uppercase convention; it does not establish that the family has only 53 biologically relevant positions or that every other column is disposable. A transformation into model coordinates needs an explicit rule and a mapping back to the original alignment. [Pfam format explanation](https://pfam-docs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/pfam-faq.html#what-is-the-difference-between-the-and-characters-in-your-full-alignments).

The conservation denominator also needs to be explained in the report. For example, stored column 37 contains 40,299 cysteine observations and 3,324 gaps. Its cysteine frequency is 100% among uppercase standard amino-acid observations, but approximately 92.38% across all 43,623 records. Both values are represented correctly in the CSV. Describing a position as fully conserved without naming the denominator would hide that distinction. These are also raw record frequencies; the report has not demonstrated a correction for sequence redundancy, so the duplicates matter when interpreting conservation.

The shown duplicate assertion checks whether a sequence belongs to a group with multiplicity greater than one. That includes the first member of every duplicated group. It therefore does not, by itself, validate the separate claim of 18,333 records beyond first occurrences. That quantity should be checked as the total record count minus the number of distinct sequence strings, with case and gap treatment documented. This is a distinction to verify in the full code, not evidence that the reported number is wrong.

There is also an unresolved connection to the shared project plan. The PDF repeatedly labels this pipeline as Week 5 and uses Kunitz/PF00014, whereas the local working plan and existing reports use chorismate mutase and describe sampling from an existing trained model. The week labels might refer to different schedules, but the benchmark choice needs an explicit shared decision. An alignment and conservation table do not supply the trained single-position and pairwise parameters expected by the sampler. Before adopting Kunitz, the team needs to identify a compatible pretrained model or explicitly revise the plan to include obtaining or fitting one.

For the next handoff, I would ask for the original download URL and release/date, its checksum, both complete scripts, the generated outputs, and the command and log for the validation run. Alongside those files, the biology lead should provide a reference sequence, a map from stored columns to retained model positions and biological residue numbers, proposed residue restrictions with biological reasons, and the relevant assay information. Those deliverables would let the computer lead connect the analysis to a clearly defined sampling input. A short live walkthrough of one raw record, one position statistic, and one validation check would also make the method easy to review together.

The strongest supported conclusion is that there is a useful, internally consistent dataset-inspection artifact. The report does not yet establish a complete biological benchmark, a trained target model, successful sampling, or end-to-end reproducibility. Its progress is worthwhile, and the next step is to turn that descriptive analysis into the biological decisions and documented inputs the implementation requires.
