Broad-reference results

The larger run needs more precise estimates.

The small case tells us the comparison works, but we also need a trustworthy reference for the broader target.

Our larger direct run produced 131,072 observations in about 202 seconds.

  • Its energy effective sample size reached 5,833, which cleared the required minimum of 4,000.
  • However, the mean uncertainties for energy and distance were still too large.
  • The frequency checks also left 121 uncertainties above the limit and 11 unknown.

The next step is to bring those uncertainties within the limits required for the reference.
