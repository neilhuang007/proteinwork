The sampling target

The energy defines a distribution over sequences.

We describe those preferences and interactions with a Potts model, which assigns each sequence an energy.

We then turn that energy into a probability using the relationship below.

π(x) ∝ exp(−β E(x)).

In words, lower-energy sequences are more likely, and the inverse temperature β controls that preference.

We still want a range of sequences, so our goal is to sample this distribution rather than minimize energy.

This distribution is the common target for both sampling methods.
